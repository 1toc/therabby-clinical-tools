# Code of Conduct

This project aims to support respectful collaboration among clinicians, developers, educators, researchers, and community members.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully
- assume good intent while addressing harm directly
- avoid discriminatory or demeaning language
- respect patient privacy and professional boundaries
- avoid sharing identifiable clinical information without appropriate permission

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, disclosure of private information, and unsafe clinical claims.
